Who this is

课猫 Echo is an English and Chinese speaking-practice app made by Dibya Jyoti Sarangi (Play Console developer name Physicist_who_develops). This page covers the Android app com.ayk.echo and the same app on the web at dsarangi7.github.io/Echo and echo-psi-ashen.vercel.app. The privacy policy is here (dsarangi7.github.io/Echo/privacy).

There is no account to delete

There is no sign-in and no developer-run server that stores your practice. The developer does not keep a copy of your streak, reminder time, word-deck progress, theme, or microphone audio. This page does not send a deletion request to a server. You delete the on-device data yourself, using the steps below. Email is only a way to ask for help.

Delete data on this device

These records stay in the browser or the Android WebView on the device:

  • Language, open card, and Hear it pace (echo-practice-lang, echo-practice-index, echo-hear-pace).
  • Streak and Shanghai practice days (echo-practice-streak).
  • Reminder on/off and clock time (echo-practice-reminder), and whether the test notification already fired (echo-reminder-test-fired).
  • Office words progress (echo-office-words).
  • Theme, landing, mode, tips (echo-color-theme, echo-landing-dismissed, echo-app-mode, echo-match-tip, echo-whisper-interstitial).
  • The open-visit tally in session storage (echo-session-tally). It already disappears when the tab or process closes.
  • IndexedDB echo-practice-sync, a local copy of the streak and reminder.
  • The on-device speech model in the Cache API (transformers-cache), and, on the website, the service-worker cache of the app shell and audio.

Website. In the browser settings for this site, clear site data (cookies and site data, or “clear storage”). That removes the keys above. Closing the tab only removes echo-session-tally.

Android app. Uninstall 课猫 Echo, or open the phone’s Settings → Apps → 课猫 Echo → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by phone). There is no in-app account screen that deletes a server copy, because there is no server copy.

Android backup

The Android app sets android:allowBackup="true". If Android backup is turned on, Google may keep a copy of the app’s local data, including WebView storage, in your own Google account. The developer cannot read that backup and cannot delete it. To remove it, turn off backup for this phone, or delete the app’s backup from your Google account (Google account → Data and privacy → backup, or the backup controls your phone shows). Uninstalling the app does not always remove a backup Google already stored.

Translate and the microphone

Translate is optional. Practice and word decks do not call it. When you translate, this device sends the sentence (at most 500 characters) and a language pair (en|zh-CN or zh-CN|en) to MyMemory at https://api.mymemory.translated.net/get. The developer does not keep a copy and cannot erase MyMemory’s logs. For that, contact MyMemory. Their terms: mymemory.translated.net/terms-and-conditions.

Microphone audio for Say it, and for Speak on Translate, is transcribed on this device and is not uploaded. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app removes the speech-model cache. It cannot delete audio the app never stored.

没有可删除的账号

没有登录，也没有开发者运营的服务器在保存你的练习。开发者不留一份你的连续天数、提醒时间、单词卡进度、外观或麦克风录音。 本页不会向服务器发送删除请求。请按下面的步骤自行删除设备上的数据。电子邮件只是求助方式。

删除这台设备上的数据

这些记录留在这台设备的浏览器或 Android WebView 里：

  • 语言、当前卡片和「听一听」语速（echo-practice-lang、echo-practice-index、echo-hear-pace）。
  • 连续天数和上海日历中的练习日（echo-practice-streak）。
  • 提醒开关和时钟时间（echo-practice-reminder），以及测试通知是否已响过（echo-reminder-test-fired）。
  • 办公词汇进度（echo-office-words）。
  • 外观、开场、模式和提示（echo-color-theme、echo-landing-dismissed、echo-app-mode、echo-match-tip、echo-whisper-interstitial）。
  • 会话存储里这一次打开的计数（echo-session-tally）。关闭页面或进程后它本来就会消失。
  • IndexedDB echo-practice-sync，连续天数和提醒的本地副本。
  • Cache API 里的本地语音模型（transformers-cache）。网站上还有 Service Worker 缓存的应用外壳和音频。

网站。 在浏览器里打开本站的设置，清除网站数据（Cookie 和网站数据，或「清除存储」）。上面的键会被删掉。只关闭页面，只会去掉 echo-session-tally。

Android 应用。 卸载课猫 Echo，或在手机的设置 → 应用 → 课猫 Echo → 存储 → 清除存储（不同手机的用词可能不一样）。应用里没有账号页面可以删除服务器上的副本，因为没有那份副本。

Android 备份

Android 应用设置了 android:allowBackup="true"。如果打开了 Android 备份， Google 可能把应用的本地数据（包括 WebView 存储）留在你自己的 Google 账号里。 开发者读不到这份备份，也不能删除它。若要去掉，请关闭这台手机的备份，或在你的 Google 账号里删除该应用的备份 （Google 账号 → 数据和隐私 → 备份，或手机上显示的备份控制）。卸载应用不一定会删掉 Google 已经保存的备份。

翻译和麦克风

翻译是可选的。练习和单词卡不会调用它。翻译时，这台设备把句子（最多 500 字）和语言方向 （en|zh-CN 或 zh-CN|en）发给 MyMemory： https://api.mymemory.translated.net/get。 开发者不留副本，也不能删除 MyMemory 的日志。为此请联系 MyMemory。 条款：mymemory.translated.net/terms-and-conditions。

「说一说」和翻译页「说一句」的麦克风录音在本机转成文字，不会上传。清除网站数据或卸载应用会删掉语音模型缓存。 应用从未保存的录音，无法再删。
